About

I'm Platon, and I split my time between two things that don't usually end up on the same page. I draw in ink and pen, mostly by hand, and the drawings tend to wander somewhere dark. I also build 3D scenes from scratch, which is really where I get to build the rooms those drawings could live in.

Most of what's on this site started on paper or inside Blender and ended up doing something it wouldn't do sitting still, turning slowly or reacting to wherever your cursor happens to be. I like work that holds its ground the way a piece on a gallery wall does, and I like it even more when that piece can look back at you a little.
